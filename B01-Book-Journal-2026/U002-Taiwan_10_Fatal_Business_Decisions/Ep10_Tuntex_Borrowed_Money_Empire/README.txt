Tuntex episode 10 dynamic reading package

Open index.html first. It is the cover page, matching the C01 Weiquan final website structure. Choose 開始閱讀 to open website/index.html.

To connect the two YouTube buttons, edit VIDEO_URLS.captioned and VIDEO_URLS.clean near the end of index.html and paste a complete https://www.youtube.com/... or https://youtu.be/... address.

The website folder contains the C01-style player page, CSS, JavaScript, timeline data and WebP scenes. The audio folder contains the MP3 narration. All local assets use relative paths for offline playback.
